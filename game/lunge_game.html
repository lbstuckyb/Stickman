Clips driving the figure

Every frame above is 15 joint positions — where a COCO-17 detection actually put the hips, knees, ankles, shoulders, elbows, wrists and head. They are hip-centred and multiplied by one scale factor that sizes the subject into this arena; nothing is rebuilt at runtime. So the lunge covers the ground the subject covered, drops as far as the body dropped, and foreshortens the way the real limbs did. Only the walk is invented — and it is solved onto the same measured bone lengths, so nothing changes shape between states.

How scoring reads the pose

The hitbox is read off the measured pose, not off the sprite: circles at the lead ankle and lead wrist, live only during the move’s strike window. The strike sweeps forward with the step-out, so range is what the subject’s own reach makes it. You have to be in range and time the press. Each hit raises the combo; letting a target cross the far edge resets it.
